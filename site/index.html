    03The thesis

    Constructive on NEAR, as a bet on private settlement. Conditional on what the chain keeps showing.

    The price is a growth price.

    NEAR's market value is , the revenue that reaches its wallets in a year. That revenue covers of net new supply. Nobody holds NEAR for today's cash flow.

    The float is the growth signal that cannot be reported into existence.

    Volume can be restated by whoever keeps the table. A balance in a public contract cannot. If the float and NEAR's revenue wallets stall while reported volume climbs, believe the chain.

    The same float is the largest unpriced risk.

    It is described as "backed 1:1" by the treasury. The treasury is public. What the private chain owes is not, and nothing published proves the two match. A shortfall would stay invisible until a withdrawal failed.

    05Check it yourself

    No key, no account, nothing to install. Python and a public node.

    make test

    Unit tests. No network. About a second.

    make check

    Rebuilds every number on this page from the raw readings and compares. No network.

    make verify

    Re-reads a sample of days from NEAR mainnet first, then does the same. About a minute.

    Each reading is tied to a block height, so it never changes and anyone gets the same bytes. A read that fails is stored as unknown and left out. It is never counted as zero.
