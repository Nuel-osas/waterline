01What the float shows

It compounds.

First reading with a balance: 19 Feb 2026. $49.3M at the B2B launch in July. $136.4M on the pinned day, in stablecoins, NEAR, ZEC, bitcoin and ETH.

    Every token the treasury holds that has a price, daily, at the prices of the pinned day. 30 token types have no price and are left out.

    Part of it is paid for.

    On 11 June NEAR announced a reward for keeping money on the private side, with the snapshot taken once it held $70M. On 17 September NEAR said that had happened. The float grew $7.9M per 30 days before the programme, $14.3M per 30 days while the first reward was open, and $31.8M per 30 days since. A second round is promised, so nobody outside can separate use from reward-seeking.

    • Float without NEAR's own token
    • NEAR's own token in the float

    Without NEAR's own token the float is $92.9M, up from $46.1M at the snapshot, and its worst day ever is a fall of $2.7M. Of the NEAR that arrived around the snapshot, 69% has left.

    It arrives in lumps.

    The ten best days add up to $75.5M, which is 55% of the float. 12.6M NEAR entered in two weeks of September. 3.9M NEAR left in one day.

    Change in the float from one reading to the next. The ten largest rises are highlighted.

    The switch itself moved little.

    Across 27 October the float changed by 2.8%. Swaps pass through the private chain and leave. The float measures money at rest, not activity. In the 17 days since, it has added $20.7M.

    Daily float around the day 1Click swaps began to settle privately, on an axis that starts at zero.

    The reading caught a real shortfall.

    On 2 readings in October, the ledger credited the private chain with more of one dollar token than NEAR Intents held: $3.3M missing, 98% of the claim. We flagged it before we knew why. It matches a published exploit of the token's bridge to the hour, and it was whole again on 3 Oct 2026.

    03The thesis

    A small position: an option on private settlement. Conditional on what the chain keeps showing.

    265x

    The price needs growth.

    NEAR's supply is worth $4.48B, 265x the revenue that reaches its wallets in a year. That revenue covers 15% of net new supply and would have to grow 6.5x to match it. Over the latest 30 days it is -20%.

    3.5x

    The float is the adoption measure that cannot be misreported. It can be rented.

    A table can be restated by whoever keeps it. A balance in a public contract cannot. But a reward can put balances there for a while, so we watch the float without NEAR's own token: $92.9M, 3.5x since the July launch.

    $136.4M

    The same float is an unpriced risk, and one key holds it.

    It is described as "backed 1:1" by the treasury. The treasury is public. What the private chain owes is not, and nothing published proves the two match. October showed backing can fail: one token was 98% unbacked for two readings. The treasury is an ordinary account with 1 key.

    04What would change our mind

    Five readings as of the pinned day, written down then, checked again on 31 March 2027.

    A fifth item has no threshold: if NEAR publishes a proof that the private chain's balances add up to the treasury, the risk above mostly goes away.

    05Check it yourself

    No key, no account, nothing to install. Python and a public node.

    make test

    Unit tests. No network. About a second.

    make check

    Rebuilds every number on this page from the raw readings in memory and compares. No network, writes nothing.

    make verify

    Re-reads a sample of days from NEAR mainnet first, then does the same. One to three minutes.

    Each reading is tied to a block height, so it never changes and anyone gets the same bytes. A read that fails is stored as unknown and left out. It is never counted as zero. The checks write nothing, and they fail if any number on this page or in the thesis is not what the data produces.
